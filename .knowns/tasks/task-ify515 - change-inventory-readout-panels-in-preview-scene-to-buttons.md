# Change inventory/readout panels in preview scene to buttons

This will help with improving the readability of the preview scene/panel. The information about what's been mined and the current inventory/contents of the target object should be hidden behind two buttons - "Target info" and "Inventory" respectively. The "Inventory" button should open a panel that shows like a storage locker or something, with rock-type objects/sprites that cover space depending on the capacity mined...and then a simple textual explanation of what's been mined. The target info should show basically the same panel, but the difference is it's opened by a button rather than persistently being displayed
