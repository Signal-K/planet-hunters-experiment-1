# Spec: Character Roles & Management
**Status:** Deferred (Revisit in 2 weeks)
**Integration:** Star Sailors Ecosystem / Pixel Starships

### Core Concept
Characters (Crew) can be recruited using Francs or found during exploration. They are assigned to specific rooms in a Rocket to provide stat buffs.

### Roles & Buffs
- **Engineer:** Assigned to Engine Room. Reduces fuel consumption by 10-25%.
- **Miner:** Assigned to Mining Laser Room. Increases material yield or reduces laser heat buildup.
- **Scientist:** Assigned to Sensor Lab. Increases XP gain from TESS classifications and discovery probability.
- **Pilot:** Assigned to Cockpit. Reduces transit time between Earth and anomalies.

### Visualization (Godot)
- Characters should appear as simple animated sprites within their assigned rooms.
- Interaction allows viewing their level and skills.

### Progression
- Characters gain "Role XP" by being active during missions.
- Higher levels unlock "Special Abilities" (e.g., "Overdrive Drill").
