# Gameplay Vision: 20 Questions & Answers

This document records the foundational vision for Planet Hunters gameplay as established in the "20 Questions" exercise.

## Gameplay Progression
1. **Progression beyond Mission 3:** 
   - Users can visit planets, asteroids, etc. 
   - Long-term: settlements, expeditions, off-Earth research.
   - Short-term: simple mining and building (contractor-driven).
   - Mechanic: contractors may have cooldowns; users might need multiple contractors.

2. **Infinite Content:**
   - Players don't run out of rockets; they buy more.
   - Missions 1-3 are tutorial/scripted.
   - Later missions: optional, some may not be profitable, some require research (reusable rockets).

3. **Scanner Station Role:**
   - Central in early game to find new targets.
   - Optional for known targets (e.g. Kepler-22b).
   - Long-term: autonomous discovery validation (collaboration with other users).

4. **Difficulty Scaling:**
   - Classification difficulty is naturally variable based on the dataset.

## Long-term Citizen Science
5. **Real-world feedback:** Long-term integration of discovery confirmations.
6. **Scientific use:** Data to be transmitted to interested parties (astronomers).
7. **Community progress:** Future indicators (e.g. "12,000 targets classified").
8. **Persistence:** Data stays in Supabase.

## Construction & Sandbox
9. **Construction:** Settlements, mining stations, relay stations/outposts.
   - Sandbox UX similar to Crashlands.
   - Crafting & refineries/furnaces.
10. **Multiplayer:** Async collaboration (sharing/requesting help on discoveries). No real-time visitations yet.
11. **Resources:** Mined resources used for building or sold to market.
12. **Cooperative:** No destructive competition.

## Star Sailors Ecosystem
13. **Relationship:** Minigame within the Star Sailors ecosystem.
14. **Lore:** Shared accounts/universe.
15. **Currency/XP:** XP shared across ecosystem; Francs are siloed.
16. **Identity:** Same database; profiles/achievements across games.

## Launch & SHIP-ing
17. **Platforms:** Web, mobile PWA.
18. **Minimum Feature Set:** To be decided by agent based on feel.
19. **Legal:** No major IRB/GDPR blockers for classifications.
20. **Target Player:** Combination of Star Sailors users, casual gamers, and citizen science enthusiasts.
