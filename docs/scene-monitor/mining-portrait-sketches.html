Portrait mining sketches

Three ways to rebuild laser mining so it is made for an upright phone, not squeezed into one. Each frame below is playable: drag inside it to steer, press its round button to fire. Rough sketches, not final art.

Why today's scene fails upright

  • It is a sideways scroller. Ore arrives from the right, so the screen's narrow side is the look-ahead. At 390px you see about 2 deposits and a new one roughly every 5 s.
  • The tall side is spent on sky. The ship sits at the top and the ground near the bottom, so the beam crosses about 430px of empty space. That is why shots have to be led and why the aim line has to predict where the seam will be.
  • The controls take a quarter of the height and need two thumbs, with a four-way d-pad where only left and right matter.
  • Labels crowd the action ("Leonid Debris" floats over the ship).

A · Top-down pass

Recommended
Drag to steer · FIRE hits the reticle

Look straight down. The ground flows down the screen under the ship, so the tall side becomes look-ahead: you see ore about 4 s before it reaches you. The beam hits a reticle just ahead of the ship, instantly, so there is nothing to lead. One thumb steers anywhere on the screen; the other fires.

Keeps everything the current run has: flying, weaving, the one-minute goal, the CATCH button for showers (fragments fall in from the top). Level 2 ore shows as dim seams the starting laser cannot cut.

B · Cutaway

Drag to slide · hold CUT to burn down

The screen is a slice through the ground: surface near the top, rock below. The ship slides along the surface and holding the button burns a shaft straight down until it reaches ore or the laser's reach. The phone's height becomes depth, which is exactly what laser levels are about: the dashed line is how deep your laser reaches.

Strong fit for laser upgrades and the "nothing is entered" rule. Slower and more deliberate, so it needs the heat limit to keep the minute busy. Least like today's scene.

C · Spinning rock

One button · fire when a seam is under the beam

The ship holds over a small body that turns beneath it. Seams on the rim pass under the beam; fire as one lines up. The spin speeds up and reverses so it is not one rhythm. Fits any screen, works one-handed.

Simplest to build and play, and a nice fit for asteroids. Loses steering, so the "weaving" rule becomes timing only, and it reads less like a planet's surface.

What I'd do

  1. Build A in the stage fixture as a new mining layout, portrait first, reusing the controller's ore, mineral and charge rules. Landscape gets the same top-down view with the ship lower-centre.
  2. Replace the d-pad with drag-to-steer in mining. That changes the shared mining and rover control rule (2026-10-30), so it needs your yes.
  3. Keep B in mind for laser level 2 in cycle 9; its depth line could appear as a deeper seam band in A.
