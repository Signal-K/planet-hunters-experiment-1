# Cycle 7 goals

Liam, 2026-10-29, looking back over the cycle: the key goals turned out to be two.

1. **Community research.** A standing, shared reason to play: weekly Explorer and Trader
   paths, sky events that feed it, and community instruments everyone helps launch and use.
2. **Launch anywhere, always something to do.** A player can launch a rocket and send it
   anywhere, and there is always something useful to do: mining, selling resources, building
   probes and instruments, rover trips. Community research is one of those options, not the
   only one.

Work that serves neither goal waits for a later cycle.
