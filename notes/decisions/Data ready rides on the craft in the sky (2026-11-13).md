# Data ready rides on the craft in the sky (2026-11-13)

Liam's iPad notes of 13 November (SSL-834) crossed out the "1 DATA READY" pill in the Base HUD, with arrows pointing to the craft in the sky.

- Data waiting at the Data hub is shown on the craft that collected it, as a tag that flies with that craft across the Base sky. It is not shown as a HUD pill.
- A craft with data waiting opens the Data hub when tapped. A craft with no target this week opens pointing first.
- An instrument with no craft overhead, such as the Saturn imager, puts its data on the first craft.
- The pill is shown only when no craft is in the sky to carry the data.
