# Landnam Minimal is one side-on view of the base (2026-11-23)

Liam, on /minimal: the old flow switched 2D/3D/2D/3D/2D (side-on base, TakeOn
rover diorama, iso launch pad, top-down fly-over, iso home) and the fly-over
controls made no sense. "It isn't minimal."

## Rules

- One perspective: side-on, sky above, ground below. Home is the base; a
  destination is the same frame in that world's colours. Only the rocket
  travels between them. No TakeOn dioramas, no iso, no top-down.
- The base is the progress. Structures start at level 1 and level up from
  missions flown; each has a counter under it. No building or buying.
- Starting something is one tap: NEXT MISSION opens a briefing sheet over the
  base, LAUNCH sends the rocket off the pad. Every unlocked mission stays
  replayable from the strip above it (the sandbox stays).
- Sheets, never new screens: briefing, structure levels and the mission report
  are cards over the base.
- One control at a destination: tap ore to cut it, or one button for the job
  (deploy, release probe, build, deliver). No steering, no FIRE button.
- Events live in the base's sky (the current meteor shower), with what opens
  them when locked.
- Minimal keeps Landnam's missions, rules and art; it never imports Landnam's
  screens or styles.
