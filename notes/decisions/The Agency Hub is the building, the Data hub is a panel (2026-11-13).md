# The Agency Hub is the building, the Data hub is a panel (2026-11-13)

Decided by Liam, from his iPad notes on the Earth Base (13.11.26, SSL-834).

- The structure beside the launchpad (`control-station` in code) is the **Agency Hub**, the agency's headquarters. It is never labelled or presented as the Data hub.
- Tapping the Agency Hub opens the **Mission log**. The Mission log is no longer a row in the Command menu.
- The **Data hub** is only a panel (`instrument-hub`). It opens from the Command menu and from the data-ready indicator. It never becomes a building on the Base.

Supersedes the "Data hub" label on the built station from earlier cycles. The `control-station` id stays, so saves do not change.
