# Community research is the ecosystem scorecard and release is public (2026-11-06)

Decided by Liam, after the release plan.

## Decision

- The first release is public. Everything is public; there is no invite-only phase.
- Community events and research, as in Pokémon Go or No Man's Sky, are the big scorecard. They track what players do across the whole Star Sailors ecosystem (Landnám, the rover game, the solar game and later games), not only Landnám.
- The scorecard gives players a clearer pathway: what the community is working on now, what each game contributes, and where to play next. Liam: "I think", so treat the exact shape as a hypothesis to test with real players.
- It extends, and does not replace, `Community research is the standing event (2026-11-01)`: the 100-stamp monthly goal and its bonuses stay.

## Consequences

- Every game reports contributions to one shared community record, so a rover classification or a solar contribution counts beside a Landnám stamp. Each game keeps its own small concept; the scorecard is the thing that ties them together.
- Public launch means the release checklist covers abuse and load: server-light reads (`fields=`, paging, no polling), moderation for anything player-written, and sign-in required.
- Landnám's job at release is to be the front door: from the scorecard a player can see which game suits them.

## Open

- What counts as a contribution in each game and how they are weighted on one scorecard.
- Whether the scorecard lives in Landnám, in a shared site, or in each game with one shared server record.
