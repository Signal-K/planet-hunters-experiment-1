# Two-tone mission style is the UI standard (2026-10-29)

Decided by Liam during the Orionids work (SSL-714).

## Decision

- Mission and research screens use the two-tone style first built for Community research:
  the scene split into two translucent coloured halves over the world, dark translucent panels
  with large rounded corners, cream text, cyan accents, gold for rewards, and the cream
  contract button as the main call to action.
- The colour pair can change per screen when it carries meaning (the ORI-1 radio is radio blue
  and meteor red); the structure stays the same.
- The old white, square panels, dialogs and buttons are retired. Any screen still using them is
  moved to this style when it is next touched. Never add new ones.
- Keep text short: one idea per panel, a sentence or two. If a panel needs a paragraph, it is
  doing too much.

## Reference

Community research, the ORI-1 meteor radio, the mission debrief and the mining storage choice
(cycle/7, 2026-10-29).
