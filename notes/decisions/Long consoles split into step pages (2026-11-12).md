# Long consoles split into step pages (2026-11-12)

Liam, on the survey probe console (SSL-773): "this is beautiful, but it really isn't passable. Requires too much scrolling. Maybe dividing things into steps/pages is better, especially on mobile."

Rule: a console where the player works through several stages (pick, read, mark, log) shows one stage per page, with a numbered step strip (`1 Pick · 2 Read · 3 Logged`), not every panel stacked at once. Each page must fit a 390×844 phone with no scrolling, and the same pages are used on iPad and desktop.

- Every page has one main button that moves to the next step, plus a way back (BACK TO MAP).
- Only the tools for the current step are on screen. The map and the spectrum are never shown together.
- First applied to the probe console's reading activity. That activity has moved to cycle 11's scan levels (see `Basic probe scan is automatic; scan levels in cycle 11 (2026-11-12).md`), which must follow this rule.
