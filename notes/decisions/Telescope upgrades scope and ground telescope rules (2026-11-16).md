# Telescope upgrades scope and ground telescope rules (2026-11-16)

Decided by Liam (16 Nov). Ticket: SSL-802.

## Decision

- Order of work: more ground telescopes first, then telescope levels 1–3, then one Upgrade flow for every scope.
  Upgrading a probe already in orbit comes later.
- "Look further" means a telescope reaches more distant targets for missions.
- An extra ground telescope costs ₣2M, one per annex slot (two slots today, so two telescopes at most).
- A ground telescope observes once it stands on an annex slot. The one a player built before this rule observes
  from the Unplaced tray too; telescopes built since must be placed first.
- Early telescope upgrades cost francs only. Mineral upgrades for special features arrive in cycle 12.

## Why ₣2M holds

One telescope adds one asteroid candidate a day (15 research XP, about ₣3–4M of research value going by rocket
research prices). The two-slot cap and the ~310 XP of research sinks bound the gain. If a third slot or telescope
is ever added, price each extra telescope higher (for example ₣2M, ₣4M, ₣8M).
