# Rocket workshop weekly parts

SSL-850 asks for a physical stack, weekly research parts, livery and live mission checks. Liam explicitly schedules iOS for cycle 13 in the ticket comment (19 November).

Implementation choices for the web slice: existing weekly milestones 2, 4 and 5 grant permanent access to Storage Silo Bank T2, Fusion Thruster T2 and Ringed Mining Laser T3 respectively, alongside their existing fragments and badge. These reuse the existing catalog and balance values. They fit for free, regardless of mission-count unlocks, and do not generate refunds when removed. Ordinary parts retain their existing price-delta/refund rule. Previously claimed milestones and weekly breakthrough badges migrate to these entitlements. Repeated weeks do not duplicate a part.

Hull and trim use shared-token colours, with none, star, orbit or stripes decals. Paint is free and saved immediately. Livery is rendered in the workshop; the existing launchpad/flight sprites keep their current art. Fitted parts affect actual cargo capacity, orbit reach, mining laser charges and laser tier. The workshop names each failed mission limit and checks the farther delivery leg too; launching stays on the launchpad.
