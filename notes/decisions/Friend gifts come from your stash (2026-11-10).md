# Friend gifts come from your stash (2026-11-10)

Decided by Liam (SSL-794).

## Decision

- A player can give each friend, once a day, 3 of any common or uncommon ore or 1 of any rare or exotic ore.
- The ore comes out of the sender's stash. The sender gets a research stamp (`gift`). The old random roll created ore and francs from nothing.
- Currency and blueprint gifts are retired. Gifts already sent can still be claimed.
- Shower debris and fireball fragments cannot be given.
- Open: the `gift` stamp is in the research ledger but does not fill the shared monthly bar (research.go counts only the first four kinds).
