# Shower special missions: gold opens more than silver (2026-11-12)

From Liam (22 Oct, SSL-657): a meteor event unlocks later special missions on that shower. Which ones depends on whether the player did it in the window (gold) or after it (silver). Unlocks stack like a season pass.

## The keys and what each opens

| Key | Opens | Body | Pays |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Event badge (silver or gold) | Impact survey | Itokawa | ×1.3 |
| Gold badge only | Pristine recovery | Psyche | ×1.8 |
| Meteorite Mastery 1 (plus the badge) | Meteorite assay | Bennu | ×1.5 |
| Orionids find voucher | Voucher run | Vesta | ×1.5 with a gold voucher, ×1.2 with silver |

- Pays: each mission pays the value of what it mines, times the multiplier.
- Stacking: every Meteorite Mastery level adds 10% to every special mission, up to three levels.

## Rules

- Each special mission is a Free run on a real body tied to meteorites: no client and no fee. It is flown once.
- The shower's Events card lists every special mission. Locked ones show what they need, and an open one starts straight on the Free run tab.
- Code: `web/lib/orionids/special-missions.ts`.
