# Mining missions follow one scene flow (2026-10-29)

From Liam's Landnam Scene Flow Proposal board (Freeform, 29.10.26, Fotografiska Tallinn).
Only the parts that agree with the current rules are recorded here; the open questions at the
bottom are still Liam's to decide.

## Decision

- Everything starts at the player's Earth hub. A mission is: Hub, Map, Review and Launch,
  Transit, the destination (orbit or surface), the work, the return, the debrief. This is the
  five-stop loop (Plan, Launch, Flight, Work, Result) and adds no new stop.
- Picking a mission first or a destination first ends on the same Review and Launch screen:
  destination facts on one side (name, location, distance), the missions available there on
  the other, Back and Launch. It is one screen, not a two-panel display followed by a review.
- The map opens at any time. It shows only bodies the player owns or can reach, and highlights
  the ones that suit the picked mission. The first map is the Solar System (inner, and inner
  plus outer); a galaxy level waits until there is somewhere beyond it to go.
- Mining has two methods: the ship's laser from orbit (the default, unchanged) and a rover on
  the surface. The method is picked before launch. What the haul is for (a client contract,
  selling at market, or the player's own project) is settled at the debrief, not before.
- A client contract names its target; the map highlights it and the player does not search
  for one. Delivering the cargo and putting the rocket away are one debrief moment, with the
  client taking delivery as the animation.
- Scanning never gates mining. An unscanned body is still mineable; a quick scan shows how
  rich its deposits are. The full citizen-science scan (one a day) is cycle 8.
- When Review and Launch asks for something the player lacks, it offers "Where to get it",
  which opens the map with the sources highlighted. This is the mission guide; it is not a
  separate system.
- Mining settlements (semi-autonomous pits mined level by level) are cycle 9 and not built
  before then.

## Answered by Liam (2026-10-30)

- A haul can be stored in silos on the planet it was mined on, and a rover can be left on site
  for the next trip. This brings back the remote silo removed on 2026-10-24.
- Contract cargo is delivered to Earth unless the contract says otherwise (Belt Courier Run
  says Vesta, so it keeps Vesta).
- Rover surface mining uses signal-k/takeon (the Takeon rover game Landnam already mounts).
- The mission guide ("Where to get it") waits; no mission consumes held resources yet.
