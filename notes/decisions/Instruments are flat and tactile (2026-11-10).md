# Instruments are flat and tactile (2026-11-10)

Liam, 10 Nov: the instrument screen should be colourful but not use a massive gradient. It should feel tactile but not this complex. It was also too pink, too purple and too cramped.

- Console panels are solid colour with a cream edge and a hard drop shadow. No glass, blur, iridescent rim or glow layers.
- Keys are chunky and sink when pressed.
- Colour comes from the instrument finish: `a` is the accent, `b` fills the main key, `c` is the one warm highlight. The default (level 1) finish is blue with amber.
- Pink and purple are not used in the default finish.
- The readout panel keeps room: text 14px or more, taps 48px, no step hidden behind the answer key on a short landscape phone.
