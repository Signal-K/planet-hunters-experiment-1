# Ground telescope upgrade costs (2026-11-20)

Asked for the missing level-up prices in SSL-855, Liam answered in chat: “Maybe one basic mineral, then two”. Use 1 U iron for level 2 and 2 U iron for level 3. Iron is the starter ore. This supersedes the 16 Nov franc-only plan for ground telescope upgrades; extra telescopes still cost ₣2M.

Each owned telescope upgrades independently, even in the Unplaced tray. Levels 1, 2 and 3 supply 1, 2 and 3 asteroid candidates per observing day, using the existing daily digest. Placement and the grandfathered first-scope exception remain intact. Customize lists every owned ground telescope, regardless of camera position or placement, and shows cost, benefit, shortfall and maximum level.

SSL-802 remains responsible for distant mission reach and a common upgrade flow for orbital instruments; this bugfix does not increase rocket range.
