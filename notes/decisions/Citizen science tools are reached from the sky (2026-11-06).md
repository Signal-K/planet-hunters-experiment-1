# Citizen science tools are reached from the sky (2026-11-06)

Decided by Liam (SSL-790).

- Citizen science tools (telescopes and the like) are reached from a dialogue in the Base sky. It appears when a tool needs positioning, or has data ready to be classified, and its button opens the tool.
- Positioning a telescope is pointing it anywhere on the whole sky. The long goal is to have mapped the entire sky. Pointing is the citizen science tooling and is never a step inside Community research.
- Community research is the screen it was before SSL-768: the path picker, the six-step path, rewards and votes. Do not replace or extend it with instrument tools.
