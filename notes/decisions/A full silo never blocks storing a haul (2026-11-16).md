# A full silo never blocks storing a haul (2026-11-16)

Decided by Liam (iPad, 16 Nov), furious that a full Surface Silo stopped him storing the aluminium he mined. Ticket: SSL-845.

## Decision

- Owning a silo means Store is always available, on the pre-mining gate and on the Cargo home sheet. It is never greyed out for being full.
- The haul the player chooses to store goes in. When it does not fit, the cheapest ore already in the silo is sold at market to make room. Only a haul bigger than the whole silo spills its own cheapest ore.
- Say it before the player confirms ("Silo full: sells your cheapest stored ore to make room", "Sells N cheapest stored"), and after ("Sold N older ore to make room").
- This replaces the old rule that ore already held is never force-sold.
