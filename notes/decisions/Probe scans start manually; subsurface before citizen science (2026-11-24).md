# Probe scans start manually; subsurface before citizen science (2026-11-24)

Liam, SSL-832: “Scanning starts off with just a manual button press, like in TSP. The actual citizen science/full-on readings don't come until level 4 of the scanning and is more integrated into the citizen science modules.”

SCAN starts timed mapping, rather than revealing a scan already running. Levels 2 and 3 add subsurface scanning: Liam approved a shallow pass followed by a deeper pass, each started with SCAN. Level 4 integrates full readings into the citizen science modules.

This supersedes the automatic-at-arrival rule of 12 November and the unapproved proposal for spectral analysis at level 2. Existing probes retain their already-started mapping and downloaded evidence. Existing cadence, control-station effects, deposit estimates and observation stamp rules remain in use.
