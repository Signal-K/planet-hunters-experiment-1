# Timed mining uses Takeon and controls never overlap

Liam, 28 November 2026, this session.

Every button must be clickable and every piece of text readable at every screen size. Overlap that prevents either is a failure.

The asteroid/comet target is a Takeon-generated, Takeon-rendered world object. Do not substitute an SVG illustration or a panel of UI blobs. Use a dedicated mining scene with a minimal header, the interactive target, and a separate instruction/action row. Keep the weekly community treasury receipt and contribution log.
