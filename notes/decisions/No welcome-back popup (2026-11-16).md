# No welcome-back popup (2026-11-16)

Decided by Liam (16 Nov). Ticket: SSL-846.

## Decision

- There is no "Welcome back · While you were away" popup or card, on web or iOS. It was removed entirely (SSL-701, SSL-702 are reverted).
- Do not reintroduce a catch-up summary on return. Things that happened while the player was away show where they live
  (the market, the Data hub, the building itself), and the daily telescope digest uses its normal toast.
