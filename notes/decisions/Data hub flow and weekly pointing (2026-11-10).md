# Data hub flow and weekly pointing (2026-11-10)

Decided by Liam, from his sketch "Landnam Data Classification Plan" (10.11.26, Game_Ideas.pdf).

**Flow, much simpler to reach, still tactile:**
1. Hub scene: a "Data available" marker on the structures. Tap it.
2. Data hub: the instrument folder on the left, the sky or world map on the right (a viewport boundary). Tap a target on the map.
3. Data view: the data with tactile controls. Desktop has the controls beside it and "your info and findings" on the right. Mobile has the data, the tactile controls and a "view next step" key.
4. Data readout: Back and Confirm.
5. After the readout the player goes back to the map. Review, adding to the map (to investigate or scan further) and a visit are all the player's own research.

It flows on from other missions: launching a telescope is what makes it appear in the data hub.

**Reviewing others' findings** is the same flow, not a separate screen. Liam: drop the separate review screen. A review counts as the player's own research (the "review" stamp, SSL-799), and consensus is a goal of community research.

**Weekly pointing (supersedes "Pointing is never a step inside Community research" from 2026-11-06):** when the player starts the week's community research, they choose the part of the sky to scan. For Saturn it is the sector of Saturn. The sky dialogue still offers pointing too.

**Cycle 10 goal:** launch more scopes and upgrade existing scopes and probes, so a player gets more data per day and can look further.
