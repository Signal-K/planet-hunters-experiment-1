# Mission Flowchart Diagrams

Current canonical flowcharts for active onboarding. M3 and anything after it are intentionally omitted until the new M3 direction is written.

## Mission 1 (M1)

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  A[Start M1] --> B[Build Launchpad]
  B --> C[Open Missions]
  C --> D[Pick M1 Iron Reserve Order]
  D --> E[Pick compatible target]
  E --> F[Confirm prebuilt Explorer]
  F --> G[Launch and mine 6 iron]
  G --> H[Return and debrief]
  H --> I[M1 complete]
  I --> J[Prospector available for purchase]
```

## Mission 2 (M2)

```mermaid
flowchart TD
  A[Start M2] --> B[Pick M2 Silicon Bulk Order]
  B --> C[Pick compatible target]
  C --> D[Purchase Prospector for 1.3B F]
  D --> E[Confirm prebuilt Prospector]
  E --> F[Launch and mine 8 silicon]
  F --> G[Return and debrief]
  G --> H[M2 complete]
```

## Boundary

Only M1 and M2 are represented here. M3 waits for the new direction.
